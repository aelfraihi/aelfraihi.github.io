About

Building the tools to hold AI systems accountable.

I am a PhD candidate in Informatics, Data & AI at École Polytechnique and Inria (LIX laboratory), working under the supervision of Oana Goga. My research focuses on the algorithmic auditing of large-scale AI delivery and targeting systems.

Using black-box audit techniques—sock-puppet experiments, controlled-variation designs, and statistical inference—I investigate how platforms personalize content and ads, with a particular focus on transparency, fairness, and regulatory compliance under the EU Digital Services Act.

My empirical findings have directly informed policy submissions to the European Data Protection Board, translating research into regulatory impact.
